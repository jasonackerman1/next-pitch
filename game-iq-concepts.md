# Game IQ — Concept List, Draft 1

*Units 1–2 are now built as cards: see `game-iq-cards-review.md` for the exact questions. "Clear the bat" was dropped from Unit 1, since the GHVBL on-deck rule means kids shouldn't be stepping into live play to grab it.*

(different wording, different format, different situation), so the app can test
the idea without him memorizing a single question.

## Unit 1 — Awareness: On Deck, In the Box & In the Dugout
*The game around you, even when you're not the play.*

1. 📘 **On deck only where it's allowed.** GHVBL allows an on-deck batter ONLY in an enclosed on-deck area. No enclosed area = wait in the dugout with your helmet on.
2. **The ball is still live when you step out.** With runners on, stepping out of the box doesn't stop the play. Don't wander off for practice swings; keep your eyes on the runners. If a runner is stealing home, get out of his path fast and predictably *(coach to confirm which way he teaches)*. *(Owen's real play: he stepped out to take practice swings, his teammate stole home, and in trying to get out of the way he got in the runner's path and the runner was called out.)*
3. **Runner coming home? You're the traffic cop.** *(Coach to confirm who does this when there's no on-deck area.)* The on-deck hitter moves behind home plate and signals the runner: hands down = slide, hands up = stand up.
4. **Know the situation before you step in.** Outs, count, runners, score. Check all four every time.
5. **Get the sign every pitch.** Missed it? Ask for time. Never guess.
6. **Study the pitcher from on deck.** How fast is his fastball? What does he throw on the first pitch?
7. **Sprint on and off the field** when the inning changes. Effort plays are free.

## Unit 2 — At the Plate: Count Smarts

1. **A foul bunt with two strikes is strike three.** So is a missed bunt. *(Owen's real play: two strikes, bunted at a high pitch, missed, out.)*
2. **Pull the bunt back on a high pitch.** High pitches pop up. Only bunt strikes, and bunt the top half of the ball.
3. **Two-strike approach.** Choke up, widen your zone, and foul off close pitches. Protect the plate.
4. **Hitter's counts (2-0, 3-1).** Look for your pitch in your zone and be ready to swing hard.
5. **3-0 means take** unless the coach gives you the green light.
6. 📘 **Dropped third strike.** If the catcher doesn't catch strike three and first base is empty (or there are two outs), RUN. If first is occupied with fewer than two outs, you're automatically out.
7. **Ball four is live.** Run hard to first and watch for a defense that's not paying attention.
8. **The sacrifice bunt.** Square early, bunt strikes only, and get the runner over. You're giving yourself up on purpose.
10. 📘 **No slash bunts.** Showing bunt and then pulling back to swing is illegal in GHVBL (12U and under). The batter is out and runners go back. Pulling the bat back and taking the pitch is fine.
9. **Dropped third strike with a runner on third.** The runner on third threatening home can freeze the defense long enough for the batter to reach first. *(Real play from his last game.)*

## Unit 3 — Outfield (Left & Right)

1. **Before every pitch, know where you'll throw** if the ball comes to you (outs + runners).
2. **Hit the cutoff man.** A low, hard throw to his chest, not a rainbow over his head.
3. **Throw ahead of the runner, never behind him.**
4. **Nobody on, single to you:** throw it to second base to keep the hitter at first.
5. **Back up every play.** Right field backs up first on infield throws. Left field backs up third on throws from the right side.
6. **Call it loud and early.** "I got it! I got it!" Center fielder has priority over the corners, and outfielders have priority over infielders going back.
7. **Ball over your head:** drop step and turn and run. Don't drift backward.
8. **Ground ball with runners on:** charge it and get it in quickly. Nobody on: get in front and block it.

## Unit 4 — First Base

1. **Get to the bag fast** on any ground ball to the infield. Heels at the edge of the bag, face the thrower.
2. **Don't stretch early.** Wait until you see where the throw is going.
3. **Stretch with your glove-side foot** toward the throw. The other foot stays on the bag.
4. **Bad throw? Leave the bag.** Stopping the ball beats getting the out.
5. **Pitcher covering first:** lead him to the bag with an underhand toss.
6. **You're the cutoff man** on throws home from right and center field.
7. **Bunt coverage:** charge the bunt. The second baseman covers first.
8. 📘 **Holding a runner** (full leadoffs and stealing are allowed in GHVBL): right foot on the front corner of the bag, and give the pitcher a target.
9. 📘 **No ball, no blocking.** A fielder can't block a base without the ball. That's obstruction.

## Unit 5 — Baserunning

1. **Run through first base** on a ground ball. Don't slide. Then look right for an overthrow.
2. **Round first on a hit to the outfield.** Make a banana turn and be ready to go.
3. **Two outs: run on anything** the moment it's hit.
4. **Fly ball, fewer than two outs:** go halfway if it might be caught. Tag up if it's deep.
5. **Tagging up:** be on the bag when it's caught, and leave after the catch.
6. **Line drive, fewer than two outs: freeze.** Don't get doubled off.
7. **Runner on second, no force:** ground ball in front of you (left side) = hold; to the right side = go.
8. **Play behind you? Look at your base coach.** Play in front of you? Trust your eyes.
9. **Getting hit by a batted ball is an out.** Know where the ball is.
10. **Steal on the pitch, not on movement.** Go when the pitcher commits to home, not on the first leg kick. A righty can still step off; a lefty's leg can still go to first. *(Owen's real play: took off on the leg lift, pitcher threw to first, easy pickoff.)*
11. **Time the pitcher all game.** Watch his windup from the dugout and base so you know when he's committed.
12. **Secondary lead.** Shuffle off as the pitch is delivered so you're moving when the ball reaches the plate.
13. **Know your lead.** A runner who isn't fast takes a lead he can get back from. Get picked off and the inning dies.
14. 📘 **Watch for fake-to-third, throw-to-first.** That pickoff move is legal in GHVBL.
15. 📘 **Infield fly rule.** With runners on first and second (or bases loaded) and fewer than two outs, a pop-up an infielder can catch is an automatic out. Runners can advance at their own risk, just like on a fly ball.
16. 📘 **Avoid contact.** Slide or go around. A hard, on-purpose collision gets you ejected. Headfirst slides are allowed at 12U.
17. 📘 **Balks.** In the regular season, each pitcher gets one balk warning. In the playoffs, there's no warning.

## Unit 6 — Reset (Mindset)
*Small for now. This unit grows from the book notes at Christmas.*

1. **Next pitch.** The last pitch is over and can't be changed.
2. **Run your Reset Play** after a mistake, before the next pitch.
3. **One mistake doesn't become two.** Thinking about the error is how the next one happens.
4. **Talk like a teammate.** Pick people up, especially after they mess up.
5. **Control what you control:** effort, attitude, and being ready.
